# Midterm Review

## Mistake 1: quen dong the img
- **My answer:** toi viet `<img src="anh.jpg">` roi dong lai bang `</img>`
- **Correct answer:** `<img>` la the rong, khong co the dong
- **Why:** the rong khong chua noi dung nen trinh duyet tu dong no ngay tai cho
- **Where to review:** ebook/03-html-elements.md, phan the rong

## Mistake 2: nham class voi id
- **My answer:** toi dat `id="card"` cho ba khoi giong nhau
- **Correct answer:** dung `class="card"` cho nhieu khoi, `id` chi dung mot lan
- **Why:** id phai duy nhat trong mot trang, neu trung thi CSS va JS deu de sai
- **Where to review:** ebook/05-css-selectors.md, chuong ve selector

## Mistake 3: duong dan tuyet doi
- **My answer:** toi ghi `src="C:/Users/Minh/anh.jpg"`
- **Correct answer:** ghi duong dan tuong doi `src="images/anh.jpg"`
- **Why:** duong dan tuyet doi chi ton tai tren may cua toi, nguoi khac mo se hong
- **Where to review:** ebook/04-links-and-paths.md, phan duong dan tuong doi
