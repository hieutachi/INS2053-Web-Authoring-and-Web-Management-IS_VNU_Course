Our Mission

CodeBreakers Club aims to create a supportive community where students can learn, build, and share technology. We believe anyone can learn to code with the right environment and mentors.

Our History

Founded in 2020 by a group of five students, the club has grown to more than one hundred active members. Along the way we have won three national programming awards and hosted dozens of workshops and hackathons.
