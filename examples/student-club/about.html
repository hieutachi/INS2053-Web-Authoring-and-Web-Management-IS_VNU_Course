Our Mission

CodeBreakers Club aims to create a supportive community where students can learn, build, and share technology. We believe anyone can learn to code with the right environment and mentors.

Our History

In this fictional example, five students started a weekly study circle in 2024. They wanted a place to ask beginner questions and practise explaining their code. The group gradually added workshops and peer reviews.
