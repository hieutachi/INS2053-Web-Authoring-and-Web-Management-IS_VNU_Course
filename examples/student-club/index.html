Who We Are

CodeBreakers Club is a student community at VNU University of Science. We bring together beginners and experienced programmers to learn, build projects, and help each other grow.

Whether you have never written a line of code or you have been programming for years, there is a place for you here.

What You Can Do Here

  • Attend weekly workshops on web development, Python, and more.
  • Join hackathons and coding competitions.
  • Work on real projects with a friendly team.
  • Meet mentors and make new friends.
